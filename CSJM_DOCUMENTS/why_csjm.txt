The QS World University Rankings Sustainability 2025 is a critical assessment that examines universities' commitment to sustainability. This yearly ranking is published by Quacquarelli Symonds (QS), a renowned global education information company. The QS World University Rankings Sustainability 2025 considers a variety of essential indicators, including academic reputation, employer reputation, faculty student ratio, citations per faculty, international students, and staff with gender diversity. This comprehensive evaluation provides valuable insights for students, researchers, universities, and governments to make informed decisions regarding higher education sustainability and the future of our planet.
